EUROPE · WAR

Crimea Bridge Falls Into the Sea

Ukraine hit the 19-kilometre bridge to Crimea for a second day running. Its road arch and a road span dropped into the strait, cutting the road link; two people were killed, the Russian-installed governor said. Rail traffic later resumed.

Zelenskyy called it a seven-stage operation over weeks against air defences, radar and jamming.

MIDDLE EAST · THE GULF

Gulf on a Knife-Edge

American jets struck a Revolutionary Guard launch site at Qeshm, reports said, as Iranian drones aimed at a US radar in Bahrain were shot down. A fourth tanker in a day caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after a mine strike; Iran said it had seized a US drone.

All sides’ claims are unconfirmed. Trump warned Iran would pay a huge price for any attack on US soil.

ASIA · DIPLOMACY

Quad Meets in Manila

The leaders of the United States, India, Australia and Japan sat down in Manila for about ninety minutes — the first Quad summit since September 2024, the first of President Trump’s second term, and the first held in a country outside the group.

Critical minerals, supply chains, sea security and shipping safety topped the agenda. Next: a first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday.
